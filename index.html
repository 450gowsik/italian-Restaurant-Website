Our Story

Founded in 2018, Olive & Ember was born from a passion for authentic Mediterranean flavors and a commitment to sustainability.

We work directly with local farmers and artisans to bring you the freshest seasonal ingredients, transformed into dishes that honor centuries-old traditions while embracing modern culinary techniques. Our wood-fired oven, sourced from a family workshop in Naples, serves as the heart of our kitchen, infusing each dish with the distinctive smoky essence that defines our cuisine.

Under the guidance of Chef Elena Rodriguez, our menu evolves with the seasons, ensuring that every visit offers something new to discover while staying true to the timeless principles of Mediterranean cooking.
